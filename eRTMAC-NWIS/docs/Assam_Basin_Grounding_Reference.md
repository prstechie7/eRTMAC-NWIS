## 2. Documented Pore Pressures & Geomechanical Stress Regimes

### A. Published Pore Pressure Gradients (Alam et al., 2019 - *Geophysical Journal International*)
*   **Normal Hydrostatic Shelf Regime:** 9.81 – 10.50 MPa/km (**1.00 – 1.07 SG EMW**, 8.34 – 8.76 ppg).
*   **Depleted Sand Regimes (Tipam & Barail Main Sands):** Due to 50+ years of depletion in Nahorkatiya, local pore pressures drop to **0.88 – 0.95 SG EMW**. When drilling with 1.15 SG mud, this creates an overbalance of >400–600 psi, triggering differential sticking.
*   **Overpressured Zones (OPZ) in Barail & Kopili:**
    *   **Pore Pressure Gradient:** **14.22 to 15.50 MPa/km** (**1.45 to 1.58 SG EMW**, **12.1 to 13.2 ppg**).
    *   **Onset of OPZ:** Typically between 2,800 m and 3,100 m MD.
    *   **Sub-Thrust Formations:** Absolute pore pressures reach **48 to 54 MPa at ~3,700 m** depth.

### B. In-Situ Stress Tensors
*   **Vertical Overburden Stress ($S_V$):** 21.7 to 23.8 MPa/km (**2.21 to 2.43 SG EMW**).
*   **Minimum Horizontal Stress ($S_h$ / LOT Fracture Gradient):** 12.0 to 21.4 MPa/km (**1.22 to 2.18 SG EMW**).
*   **Maximum Horizontal Stress ($S_H$):** 14.9 to 23.1 MPa/km; oriented predominantly **NE–SW** parallel to Himalayan and Indo-Burmese collision vectors.

## 3. Structural Dip Profiles Across OIL Fields

*   **Nahorkatiya Anticline / Dome:** Stable foreland shelf; gentle dip of **2° to 5°** (locally up to **7°** near normal faults dipping 60°–75° SE).
*   **Moran Field:** Deep shelf fault block; structural dip of **2° to 6°** (predominantly **3° to 5°** dipping SE).
*   **Baghjan Field:** Hinge zone platform; structural dip of **1.5° to 4°** dipping SSE.
*   **Kumchai Field (Arunachal Foothills):** Naga Schuppen Thrust Belt; severe tectonic deformation with limb dips of **30° to 40°** (locally **45°**), creating 80–150 m stratigraphic offsets across 500 m horizontal distance.
